# Investigation Records
These files are used to maintain a structured record of your investigation and to support the creation of events and entities within PinPoint.
## Investigation ID
At the start of the investigation, create a unique **Investigation ID**.
Use the same Investigation ID consistently throughout the entire investigation and across both record types. This provides a common reference linking information collected and recorded during the investigation.
## Investigation Log
Use 'Investigation_log_PinPoint.csv' to record investigative activity, findings and relevant observations as the investigation progresses.
An example completed record is provided in:
'Investigative_Log_PinPoint_Example.csv'
Refer to the example if you are unsure what information should be entered into a particular column.
## IOC Log
Use 'IOC_Log_PinPoint.csv' to record Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) and other relevant technical indicators identified during the investigation.
An example completed record is provided in:
'IOC_Log_PinPoint_Example.csv'
Refer to the example if you are unsure what information should be entered into a particular column.
## File Format
The structure and column headings of the supplied templates should be retained.
Files intended for upload into PinPoint **must be saved in CSV (.csv) format**. Do not upload an Excel Workbook (.xlsx) or other spreadsheet format.
Before importing a CSV into PinPoint, check that the required fields are populated and that the data remains in the correct columns.
##Working Practice
Maintain these records throughout the investigation rather than attempting to reconstruct them at the end.
It is your choice when you upload them to PinPoint, you can wait until the end of the investigation, at the end of each stage or each time you have information.
Record information accurately and consistently, and distinguish between information obtained directly from an artefact and any analytical assessment or conclusion you make from the information
